# Saga Studios

Creative universe by Victor Andrei Manolescu.

## Current production

The Cosmogony of Repairing the Stars — Volume One, English illustrated edition, full-color A4 landscape (297 × 210 mm).

The original Romanian manuscript and historical saga codex are retained under `sources/`. Current editorial decisions take precedence over those historical sources.

## Editorial direction

- Complete Volume One first; the seven-volume saga remains the larger goal.
- Opening poetry precedes the first drawing scene.
- The father is memory throughout; no present-day physical appearance, death scene or cause-of-death discussion.
- Reserve the Altar storyline for later exploration of the infinite Library.

## Project folders

- `sources/`: original supplied documents.
- `books/volume-01/editorial/`: production plan and editorial records.
- `books/volume-01/manuscript/`: English manuscript work.
- `books/volume-01/art/`: illustration direction and concepts.
- `books/volume-01/layout/`: layout specifications and future typesetting sources.
- `books/volume-01/exports/`: future reviewed reading and print files.

## Status

Source documents and editorial planning are available. The English manuscript, revised artwork and print edition are not complete. The first cover concept is superseded because the father was depicted physically present.
